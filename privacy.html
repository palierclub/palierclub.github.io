Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

Palier Club is a small company developing sculpted entrance mats. Contact: palierclub@gmail.com.

What the "Palier Club Supplier Ops" application does

Palier Club Supplier Ops is an internal tool used by Palier Club only. It connects to a single Google account owned by Palier Club (palierclub@gmail.com) through the Gmail API, in order to read supplier correspondence received in that mailbox and to send replies from it. It is not offered to the public and no other Google account is ever connected to it.

What data is accessed

Emails and attachments in the Palier Club mailbox, and the labels used to organise them. The application does not access any data from other Google users.

How data is used and shared

Mailbox content is used solely to manage Palier Club's conversations with its manufacturing suppliers. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except where a supplier is the addressee of a message we send them.

Storage and retention

Working copies of messages are kept in Palier Club's private project records for as long as the related supplier relationship is active, then deleted.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights and contact

Suppliers who correspond with Palier Club may ask what we hold about them, or ask for its deletion, by writing to palierclub@gmail.com.
